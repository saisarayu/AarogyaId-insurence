POLICY NAME: Aarogya Health Guard
INSURER: HealthCare Shield Ltd.

POLICY TYPE: Individual Health Insurance

SUMMARY:
Aarogya Health Guard provides wide-ranging coverage for hospitalization, day care procedures, diagnostic services, and post-hospitalization care. This policy is designed for individuals and families who want a balanced plan with strong outpatient support and cashless claim options.

SUM INSURED:
- ₹5,00,000 per year

PREMIUM:
- Annual premium: ₹10,800
- No claim bonus: 10% discount after each claim-free year (max 50%)

COVERAGE DETAILS:
- Room rent up to ₹5,000 per day
- Surgeon's fees, anesthesia, and consultation charges
- Nursing expenses and ICU charges
- Medicines, implants, and consumables
- Diagnostic tests while hospitalized
- Ambulance charges up to ₹2,000 per event
- Post-hospitalization treatment for 90 days

WAITING PERIOD:
- Pre-existing conditions: 24 months
- Specific illness: 12 months
- Maternity benefits: 24 months

INCLUSIONS:
- Hospitalization due to illness or accident
- Day care surgeries
- Organ donor expenses
- Emergency ambulance services
- Preventive health check-up every 2 years

EXCLUSIONS:
- Cosmetic and plastic surgery
- Dental treatment unless due to accident
- Treatment for substance abuse
- Experimental therapies and unproven procedures
- Self-inflicted injuries

CO-PAY:
- 10% co-pay for claims above ₹1,50,000

CLAIM TYPE:
- Cashless claims available at network hospitals
- Reimbursement claims accepted for out-of-network treatment

POLICY CONDITIONS:
The insured must disclose all pre-existing conditions at application. Renewal is lifelong, subject to terms and premium review. Misrepresentation of information may lead to claim rejection or policy cancellation.
