POLICY NAME: Aarogya Health Secure
INSURER: HealthyLife Insurance

SUMMARY:
This policy provides comprehensive health coverage for individuals and families. It covers hospitalization, pre- and post-hospitalization expenses, diagnostics, and wellness benefits.

COVERAGE:
- Sum insured: ₹5,00,000
- Hospitalization: up to ₹5,00,000 per year
- Day care procedures: covered
- Diagnostic tests: covered up to ₹25,000 per year

PREMIUM:
- Annual premium: ₹12,500
- No claim bonus: 10% discount after first claim-free year

WAITING PERIOD:
- Pre-existing conditions: 24 months
- Specific diseases: 12 months
- Maternity benefits: 24 months

INCLUSIONS:
- Room rent up to ₹5,000 per day
- Surgeon, anesthetist, and consultant fees
- Medicines, implants, and consumables
- Diagnostic procedures during hospitalization
- Emergency ambulance charges up to ₹2,000 per event

EXCLUSIONS:
- Cosmetic and plastic surgery
- Dental treatment unless due to accident
- Treatment for self-inflicted injuries
- Treatment for substance abuse
- Experimental therapies

CO-PAY:
- 10% co-pay for claims above ₹1,00,000

CLAIM TYPE:
- Reimbursement and cashless claims available
- Network hospitals supported nationwide

POLICY TERMS:
This policy is subject to the terms and conditions of the insurer. The insured must disclose all pre-existing conditions at the time of application. Renewal is available every year with applicable premium adjustments.
